Executive Summary

We propose a revolutionary departure from digital neural networks toward analog wave-based architectures that mirror biological intelligence. This research could unlock energy-efficient AI, better generalization, and new forms of adaptive cognition.

Funding Request: $2.5M over 3 years
Expected Impact: 10x energy reduction, new AI paradigm, 15+ publications, 3+ patents

1. Problem Statement & Motivation

Current AI systems suffer from fundamental limitations:

  • Energy inefficiency: GPUs consume 300W+ vs. brain's 20W
  • Rigid architectures: Fixed layers, discrete values
  • Poor generalization: Catastrophic forgetting, brittle behavior
  • Lack of spatial awareness: No true 3D understanding
Research Gap: No existing framework combines analog signals, 3D topology, and wave-based computation in neural architectures. This represents a $10B+ market opportunity in neuromorphic computing.

2. Wave-Based Encoding: Beyond Digital

Replace discrete weights with composite waveforms:

  • Phase-based modulation for richer information encoding
  • Frequency domain learning and interference patterns
  • Analog computation with continuous signal processing

Research Question: Can wave interference patterns encode and process information more efficiently than matrix operations? Hypothesis: Yes, with 50% reduction in computational complexity.

3. Spatial Dimensionality: 3D Neural Topology

Model networks in true 3D space with local connectivity:

  • Position-dependent weights and signal propagation
  • Local microcircuits mimicking cortical columns
  • Sparse, biologically realistic connections
Mini Demo: 3D Neural Grid
Open Challenge: How to efficiently route signals through 3D space? Current graph algorithms scale O(n²). We need O(n log n) or better for practical networks.

4. STL-Based Architecture: Physical Neural Networks

Represent network topology using 3D modeling formats:

  • Vertices = neurons, edges = connections
  • Physical simulation of signal propagation
  • CAD integration for hardware implementation
Commercial Potential: Integration with existing CAD workflows could create new markets in neuromorphic chip design. Estimated $500M opportunity by 2030.

5. Biological Validation & Parallels

Our approach aligns with neuroscience findings:

  • Brain oscillations: Alpha, beta, gamma waves carry information
  • Local connectivity: 80% of cortical connections are within 1mm
  • Spatial maps: Somatosensory, visual cortex organization

6. Technical Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges:

  • Analog computation precision: Hardware noise, drift
  • 3D routing complexity: Efficient pathfinding algorithms
  • Training instability: Continuous parameter spaces
  • Hardware implementation: Custom neuromorphic chips needed

Proposed Solutions:

  • Adaptive noise compensation using feedback control
  • Hierarchical spatial indexing (octrees, R-trees)
  • Stochastic gradient methods with momentum
  • Partnership with Intel/IBM neuromorphic teams
