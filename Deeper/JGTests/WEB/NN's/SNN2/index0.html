What this is: A small spiking neural network (C. elegans-inspired: sensory → interneuron → motor) learns to move a 1D “agent” toward a target. The entire SNN state update runs on the GPU. Learning is a minimal, local, biologically-plausible rule: Δw ∝ reward · pre_spike · post_spike.

Not handholding: no PID, no symbolic state. Sensors are spikes. Motors are spikes.
