🚀 WAT in HTML - Minimal Demo

WebAssembly Text (WAT) as a first-class citizen in the HTML/CSS/JS stack!

1. Math Module

Basic arithmetic operations:

(module (func $add (param $a i32) (param $b i32) (result i32) local.get $a local.get $b i32.add) (func $multiply (param $a i32) (param $b i32) (result i32) local.get $a local.get $b i32.mul) (func $factorial (param $n i32) (result i32) local.get $n i32.const 1 i32.le_s if (result i32) i32.const 1 else local.get $n local.get $n i32.const 1 i32.sub call $factorial i32.mul end) (export "add" (func $add)) (export "multiply" (func $multiply)) (export "factorial" (func $factorial)))

2. Graphics Module

Pixel processing for canvas:

(module (func $pattern (param $x i32) (param $y i32) (param $t i32) (result i32) ;; Create interesting pattern: (x ^ y ^ t) * ((x + y) % 256) local.get $x local.get $y i32.xor local.get $t i32.xor local.get $x local.get $y i32.add i32.const 255 i32.rem_u i32.mul i32.const 255 i32.and) (export "pattern" (func $pattern)))
